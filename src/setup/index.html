Set up Navi

Navigate Chrome with your voice. This takes about a minute.

Allow the microphone

Chrome only lets extensions ask for the microphone from a normal tab like this one, so Navi asks once here.

Keep your voice on this computer (recommended)

Newer versions of Chrome can recognize speech on-device, so audio never leaves your laptop, even while Navi listens for its name. Otherwise Chrome uses Google's speech service.

Say “Hey Navi”

Navi listens in the background, so you don't need to open anything. It stays awake while you keep giving commands and goes back to sleep after two quiet minutes, or when you say “go to sleep”.

A small caption at the bottom of the page shows what it hears and says. To see the full activity, click the Navi icon in the toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu) or press Alt Shift N.

Add an AI key (optional)

Basic commands (scrolling, tabs, opening sites, “click 5”) work right away. For everything else, add an OpenAI or Claude API key in settings.
