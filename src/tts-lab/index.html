    Chrome and this computer Free

    The voices Chrome can use right now. “On this computer” voices work offline; “online” voices send the text to an online service to speak it.

    On a Mac you can add much better ones: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices, then download a “Premium” or “Enhanced” voice. Restart Chrome and it appears here.

      Kokoro Free · on this computer

      A small, natural-sounding neural model with many voices. One download covers all of them.

        Pocket TTS by Kyutai Free · on this computer

        A newer model that streams its speech, so it starts talking quickly. About 125 MB, downloaded once; its voices appear once it's loaded.

          Piper Free · on this computer

          Light, fast voices made for offline assistants. Each voice is its own download (about 63 MB), fetched the first time you play it.

            ElevenLabs Paid

            For comparison: the premium online voice from your Navi settings. Each play uses your ElevenLabs credits.
