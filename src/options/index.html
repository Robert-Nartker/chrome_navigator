ElevenLabs (better hearing and a natural voice)

Adds speech recognition that ignores nearby conversations and background audio, plus a natural voice for Navi. Audio is sent to ElevenLabs only while Navi is awake (their standard data policy applies; zero retention needs an Enterprise plan). Get a key at elevenlabs.io (Developers → API keys) with Speech to Text, Text to Speech, and Voices (read) access. Stored only in this browser profile.

Listening

You say “Hey <name>” once to wake Navi. Distinctive names with two or more syllables trigger by accident less often.

While asleep, Navi only listens for its name, using Chrome's recognizer on this computer. ElevenLabs hears you only while Navi is awake, so it costs about $0.39 per hour awake.

Sound your computer plays itself, such as a video or podcast, is also cancelled out of the mic when Chrome supports it.

Navi uses Chrome's speech recognizer on-device when possible (Chrome 139+; download it on the setup page). With this off, Navi never sends audio anywhere.
